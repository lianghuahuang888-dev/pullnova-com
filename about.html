About PullNova

We're on a mission to eliminate tedious code review and CI/CD friction so engineering teams can focus on building great products.

Our Story

PullNova was founded in 2025 by a group of senior engineers who spent too many hours reviewing boilerplate code changes and debugging flaky CI pipelines. We asked: what if AI could handle the repetitive parts, so humans could focus on the decisions that matter?

Today, PullNova helps over 10,000 developers across startups and Fortune 500 companies ship better code, faster. Our AI engine has analyzed over 2 million pull requests, catching bugs, security vulnerabilities, and performance regressions before they reach production.

Our Mission

We believe that every engineering team deserves to ship with confidence. That means catching issues early, reducing review cycle times, and giving developers the context they need to make better decisions — all without adding friction to their workflow.

PullNova is built on three core principles:

  • Accuracy over noise: Every suggestion is context-aware and actionable. No spam.
  • Speed without sacrifice: PR analysis in under 60 seconds, without compromising depth.
  • Privacy by design: Your code is your code. We never train on it, and Enterprise customers can run fully on-premise.
