7. Web Accessibility Standards

WCAG 2.2 defines four principles: perceivable, operable, understandable, robust. Level AA conformance represents the widely adopted legal baseline below Section 508 and EN 301 549. Color contrast ratios must exceed 4.5:1 for normal text and 3:1 for large text. Keyboard-only interaction mandates visible focus indicators and logical tab order. Captions and transcripts serve multimedia content, benefiting deaf and hard-of-hearing audiences. Motion preferences honor reduced-motion OS settings for vestibular sensitivity. 9. Quantum Computation Fundamentals
