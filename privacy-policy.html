Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2, 2026

1. Information We Collect

PullNova collects minimal information necessary to provide our service: GitHub/GitLab account metadata (username, email), repository metadata, and pull request content for analysis. We do not store your source code after analysis is complete.

2. How We Use Your Data

Data is used exclusively to provide PullNova's AI code review and CI/CD optimization services. We never sell your data, and we never use your code to train AI models.

3. Data Retention

Analysis results are retained for 30 days on our servers. Source code is processed in-memory and not persisted. Enterprise customers can configure custom retention policies.

4. Third-Party Services

PullNova integrates with GitHub, GitLab, Bitbucket, Slack, and other services as configured by you. Data shared with these services is governed by their respective privacy policies.

5. Security

We employ industry-standard encryption (TLS 1.3), SOC 2 compliant infrastructure, and regular security audits. Enterprise customers can deploy PullNova entirely within their own infrastructure.

6. Contact

For privacy-related inquiries, contact us at hello@pullnova.com.
